Solutions

Choose the level of protection that fits your workforce.

LWS provides different levels of protection to support different working environments, risk profiles and response requirements.

LOWER RISK

APP-BASED PROTECTION

LWS Protect

Protection delivered through an app on the worker's existing phone, with alerting and check-in.

Appropriate where: teams already carrying phones and needing a straightforward rollout.

Explore LWS Protect

MEDIUM RISK

APP + PANIC BUTTON

LWS Protect+

App-based protection with a separate physical panic button for discreet activation.

Appropriate where: work where the phone may not be reachable at the moment it is needed.

Explore LWS Protect+

HIGHER RISK

DEDICATED SAFETY DEVICE

LWS Advanced

A dedicated device carried by the worker, independent of a personal phone.

Appropriate where: environments or policies that call for standalone, purpose-built equipment.
